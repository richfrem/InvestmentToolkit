## 🧠 Strategic Investment Thesis

### 1. The availability Moat
In the AI race, the "Time-to-Compute" is more critical than the "Cost-of-Compute". CoreWeave's ability to provide massive, collocated GPU clusters with Kubernetes-native orchestration allows AI labs to train models months ahead of those waiting for Hyperscaler capacity.

### 2. The Debt-as-Leverage Engine
CoreWeave pioneered a unique financing model: using its NVIDIA GPU allocation as collateral for massive credit facilities. While risky, this allows them to scale capex at the speed of the AI cycle without excessive equity dilution. This is a "land-grab" strategy that pays off as long as the demand-supply gap for GPUs remains wide.

### 3. Operational Alpha
Unlike general cloud providers, CoreWeave's entire stack is optimized for large-scale GPU workloads (RDMA, InfiniBand, NVLink). This leads to higher "Effective Compute" per dollar spent, creating a qualitative edge that generalized clouds struggle to match.
