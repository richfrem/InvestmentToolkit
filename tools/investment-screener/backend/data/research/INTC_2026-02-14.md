The company is currently undergoing a massive transformation (IDM 2.0) to separate its design and manufacturing arms, aiming to become the second-largest external foundry by 2030.

## Investment Thesis

**Thesis Pivot: From Turnaround to National Champion.**
The "execution risk" narrative is now superseded by structural state backing. Intel has become "Too Big To Fail" in a literal sense, with the U.S. government taking a direct ~10% equity stake ($8.9B investment) alongside $11B+ in total support. This aligns national security interests directly with shareholder value.

Furthermore, the **$5B Nvidia strategic investment** validates Intel's foundry roadmap more than any internal slide deck could. Nvidia is hedging against TSMC concentration by co-designing custom AI silicon on Intel processes.

The "Bull Case" is no longer about just catching up; it is about Intel becoming the domestic engine for the AI infrastructure boom, powered by **14A process leadership** (1.4nm-class) which is reportedly outperforming internal targets and attracting tier-one hyperscaler interest.

## Key Risks

*   **Geopolitics**: While the CHIPS Act benefits Intel, any escalation in Taiwan could paradoxically hurt Intel's supply chain (substrates) before it helps their foundry demand.
*   **Execution History**: Intel has a decade-long track record of missing process nodes (10nm, 7nm). Trust is low.
*   **AI Disruption**: The shift from CPU-centric to GPU-centric data centers is a secular headwind that Intel is struggling to counter.
