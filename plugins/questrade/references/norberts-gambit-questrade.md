# Norbert's Gambit — Questrade Submission Steps

## Before You Start

Set your account's currency preference to **"Currency of transaction"** — otherwise
Questrade may auto-convert cash on settlement, applying the exact FX spread this
technique exists to avoid, before the journal request even happens.

**Where:** Management → Account Management → Currency Settlement

## Submitting the Journal Request

**Where:** Management → My Requests → Journal Shares → New Request
(direct link: `my.questrade.com/clients/en/my_requests/journalling.aspx#newRequest`)

Journal shares between the Canadian (DLR.TO) and U.S. dollar (DLR.U) symbols — same
steps regardless of direction, just swap which symbol you're converting from/to.

1. Buy the unit matching the currency you currently hold (DLR.U if you have USD, DLR.TO
   if you have CAD) — see the main skill's direction table.
2. Once the shares have **settled** (see timing below), go to the journal request page
   and submit a request to convert those shares to the other symbol.
3. Once the journal completes, sell the converted units and withdraw/use the cash.

## Timing

- **Total: up to 5 business days**, including 1-2 days for share settlement before the
  journal request can even be submitted.
- Don't submit the journal request the same day you buy — it will need to wait for
  settlement regardless.

## Limits and Fees

- **One journal request per symbol at a time** — you can't queue multiple simultaneous
  requests for the same symbol. If you need to convert in stages, each stage must wait
  for the prior request to finish processing.
- **Fee:** free for Questrade Plus members. **$9.95/request** for everyone else, deducted
  from the account automatically after the request processes — make sure there's enough
  cash in the account to cover it, separate from the amount being converted.
