## Model scope and limits

Annual FCFF incorporates speed-to-power, firm Oracle deliveries and prospective capacity expansion. Base FY2027 revenue follows dated consensus, then grows above the prior model's terminal sales. Capex, collection delays, GAAP SBC and normalized terminal economics constrain distributable cash. Framework capacity is never booked in full. The reported Jupiter notice is a timing risk, not proof of cancellation of Bloom orders.

Time-to-power advantages and firm orders support substantially higher delivery forecasts, but commissioning, fuel, factory throughput and collections still determine cash conversion. Base requires new capacity after the announced 2 GW target; this is a forecast assumption, not installed capacity.

Backlog and pipeline: Initial Oracle 1.2 GW contracted; up to 2.8 GW master framework remains conditional. No guaranteed price floor from multi-year orders.

Adversarial risks: Permitting or Jupiter timing delays; Gas availability and competing generators/turbines; Capacity additions beyond 2 GW unconfirmed; Convertible/warrant overhang: basic-share debt-outstanding convention; separate share sensitivity; Latest balance sheet June 30: unreported Q3 cash movement not assumed.
