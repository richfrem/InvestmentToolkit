## I. Core Premise

This strategy is built on a single macro conviction: **the 2020s will be defined by two simultaneous civilisational-scale competitions**, and losing either front means losing strategic dominance for a generation.

**The ASI Race** — Control of intelligence, decision-making, and technological supremacy. No longer a commercial competition — it is a national security priority analogous to the Manhattan Project. This forces a structural shift from *"most efficient"* to *"most sovereign"*, mandating onshoring of critical technology and resilient supply chains. The CHIPS Act and executive AI orders create a powerful, state-sponsored tailwind for designated domestic champions.

**The Sovereign Finance Race** — Control of value, monetary systems, and debt monetisation via digital assets. The global financial system is being tokenised. The US counter-strategy (GENIUS Act of 2025) establishes federal stablecoin rails built on Bitcoin (store of value) and Ethereum (global settlement layer), creating a perpetual structural bid for US Treasuries.

**The Feedback Loop** — Both fronts are self-reinforcing. Winning ASI requires capital; winning Sovereign Finance provides it.

> **Investment Philosophy:** *"Builder-First, Empire-Second."* Own the foundational, physically-constrained, sovereign-critical **infrastructure** required to dominate both fronts — not the application layer built on top of it.

## II. Foundational Pillars

*The pillars are the durable structural drivers of the strategy. Pillar names and their target weights are stored in `target_portfolio.json`. This section documents the conviction logic for each.*

### Pillar 1 — The Geopolitical Driver: National Security Imperative

The state-sponsored tailwind for sovereign supply chains and domestic technology champions. Driven by the CHIPS Act, executive orders on AI infrastructure, and the Pentagon's designation of compute as a strategic resource. As long as great-power competition persists, this pillar provides a structural floor for demand.

**Conviction test:** Does the US government still treat AI compute and digital finance as national security priorities? If yes, this pillar remains intact.

### Pillar 2 — Physical Constraints: Compute & Power as Bottlenecks

ASI and large-scale digital asset systems are physical phenomena limited by two primary real-world resources: **Compute** and **Power**. Owning the providers of these scarce resources is a durable, long-term strategy regardless of which specific AI models or crypto protocols win.

- **Compute:** The full semiconductor supply chain — EDA, fabs, process control, CPUs/GPUs, memory (HBM)
- **Power:** Raw electricity for compute-intensive systems. Exponential data centre energy demand makes reliable, clean, baseload power a primary bottleneck.

**Conviction test:** Is compute still the primary constraint on ASI progress? Is power still scarce for data centres? If yes, this pillar remains intact.

### Pillar 3 — Sovereign Finance: The Inescapable Feedback Loop

The US Sovereign Finance strategy creates a self-reinforcing loop:
1. The world tokenises trillions in Real-World Assets (RWAs) on programmable rails (Ethereum)
2. RWAs require regulated, USD-pegged stablecoins as the primary settlement medium
3. Under the GENIUS Act, every dollar of new stablecoins must be backed by US Treasury bills
4. This creates a structural, perpetual global bid for US government debt — leveraging crypto to fund US fiscal policy and reinforce dollar dominance

**Conviction test:** Is the GENIUS Act framework intact? Is stablecoin adoption growing? Is RWA tokenisation expanding? If yes, this pillar remains intact.

## III. Portfolio Architecture

*The structure of the portfolio. The numerical targets live in `target_portfolio.json` — this section documents the qualitative architecture.*

### Structure: Fortified Barbell

The portfolio operates as a **"Fortified Barbell"** — two ends held together by conviction, not by correlation:

- **Stable End:** Capital-efficient compounders with durable moats and visible earnings growth (e.g., AVGO, NVDA, GOOG, MSFT). These compound regardless of which specific AI application wins.
- **Alpha End:** High-conviction, high-risk, thesis-driven special situations, turnarounds, and pure-play builders (e.g., INTC, CORZ, OKLO, CRCL, CRWV). These have asymmetric upside tied to specific thesis catalysts.
- **Strategic Reserve:** A cash position (~12–15%) providing tactical optionality to exploit market dislocations.
