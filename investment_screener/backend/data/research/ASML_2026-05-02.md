## Investment Thesis

ASML represents the ultimate bottleneck in the global technology supply chain. Without their EUV machines, it is impossible for foundries like TSMC, Samsung, or Intel to produce leading-edge chips. This absolute technological monopoly gives ASML immense pricing power, as evidenced by their incredibly stable gross margins (~52%) and net margins (~29%) across the cycle. They are highly profitable and boast a Rule of 40 score of 51.62.

However, the company's valuation has become extremely stretched. At ~47x trailing earnings, the market is treating ASML less like cyclical semiconductor equipment (which traditionally trades at 15-20x P/E) and more like a high-growth SaaS platform. The market is pricing in perfect execution of their next-generation High-NA EUV tools, which cost over $350 million each, alongside a "fab on-shoring" spending spree funded by the CHIPS Act.

While ASML is arguably the highest quality company in the semi space, our DCF model indicates the current price leaves zero margin of safety. A base case projecting a 15% revenue CAGR and maintaining 30% net margins yields an intrinsic value of $1220.98. Any delay in foundry spending or tightening of export restrictions to China (which historically made up a large portion of older DUV sales) could trigger a sharp multiple contraction. Therefore, despite the unassailable moat, the stock is a SELL purely on valuation grounds.
