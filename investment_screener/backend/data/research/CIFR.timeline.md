## Investment Thesis
Cipher Mining is a pure-play Bitcoin miner. The P/S of 30.9x vastly exceeds the typical BTC mining company range of 2-8x. This premium is unexplained by operational metrics: CIFR has negative TTM earnings (PE=0) and relatively modest revenue of $223.9M.

The forward PE of 31.6x is deeply misleading. Back-calculation: FWD EPS = $17.04/31.6 = $0.54/share. With 405.9M shares, that implies FWD net income = $219M. On ~$240M Y1 revenue, that's a 91% net margin — impossible for an operational mining business. The only explanation is unrealized BTC appreciation being counted as forward "earnings" under the 2023 FASB fair value accounting rule change. This is mark-to-market gain, not cash-generative operational income.

For DCF purposes, we use only operational mining margins (not mark-to-market): bear 5%, base 15%, bull 28%. These are through-cycle normalized rates, not the elevated peak implied by BTC at $90K+. Mean-reversion rule: TTM metrics at BTC cycle peak overstate sustainable economics.

At current prices, CIFR requires BTC to sustain $150K+ and CIFR to generate 28%+ margins — a scenario with <20% probability. SELL conviction high.
