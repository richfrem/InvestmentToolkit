## Investment Thesis
AMD has successfully established itself as the indispensable "second source" to NVIDIA.

1. **The AI Duopoly**: Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Meta, Google) desperately need an alternative to NVIDIA to maintain pricing leverage and supply chain redundancy. AMD's MI300X and upcoming MI400 architecture are the only silicon capable of filling this void, practically guaranteeing AMD a 15-20% market share floor.
2. **Operational Pristine**: Generating $6.7B in FCF with a Piotroski F-Score of 8, AMD is firing on all cylinders. The legacy business (EPYC servers, Ryzen) continues to generate massive cash flow to fund the AI R&D arms race.
3. **Margin Expansion**: As AI accelerators become a larger portion of the revenue mix, gross and net margins will expand significantly toward software-like levels, supporting a higher terminal multiple.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $401.00 (50% Prob)
AMD comfortably settles into a 20% AI market share position. Revenue compounds at a 30% CAGR over 5 years, reaching roughly $128B. Net margins expand to 25%. The market awards a 30x Exit P/E, recognizing its duopoly status. The present value indicates a solid baseline return.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $94.00 (30% Prob)
NVIDIA maintains a suffocating 95%+ market share due to CUDA software lock-in. AMD's growth slows to 15%. Margins stall at 18%. The market drastically re-rates the stock down to a 20x P/E, treating it as a standard hardware manufacturer unable to break the AI software moat.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $1,072.00 (20% Prob)
AMD's ROCm software ecosystem hits an inflection point, breaking the CUDA monopoly. AMD captures 30% of the hyperscaler AI budget. Revenue compounds at 40% for 5 years, with net margins hitting an exceptional 30%. Strong cash flows allow for a 5% reduction in share count via buybacks. The market awards a massive 40x P/E.

## Key Risks
- **Software Moat**: NVIDIA's CUDA software stack is deeply entrenched. If AMD's ROCm fails to gain rapid developer adoption, their silicon will be relegated to a niche "budget" alternative.
- **Valuation Gravity**: A 32x forward P/E leaves little room for execution missteps. Any delay in the MI400 roadmap would punish the stock.
