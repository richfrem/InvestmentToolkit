## Investment Thesis

Palantir has structurally inflected from a money-losing government contractor into a highly profitable, dual-engine enterprise AI powerhouse. The launch of its Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP) has dramatically accelerated commercial adoption, solving the company's historical challenge of long, complex sales cycles. This is evidenced by a trailing revenue growth rate of 70% and a massive expansion in net margins to 36.3%.

Financially, Palantir is firing on all cylinders. Generating over $2.1 billion in trailing free cash flow, the company is self-funding its rapid expansion. Its Piotroski F-Score of 8 indicates outstanding fundamental health across profitability, leverage, and operating efficiency metrics. The structural moat—derived from its near-monopoly in classified US government data ontology and its unique approach to enterprise AI orchestration—is arguably one of the strongest in the software sector.

The sole issue is valuation. Trading at a 228x trailing P/E and a $345 billion market cap, the market is pricing in flawless execution and years of uninterrupted hypergrowth. Our DCF model applies the "hypergrowth exception," acknowledging the 62.6% implied Y1 consensus growth and modeling a 34% 5-year CAGR with sustained 36% margins. Even with these aggressive assumptions and a premium 50x exit multiple, the intrinsic value is $122.92. The stock offers no margin of safety and carries significant multiple-contraction risk if growth decelerates faster than the market's euphoric expectations.
