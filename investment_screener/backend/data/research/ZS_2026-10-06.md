## Recommendation and confidence

The refreshed annual FCFF model produces a **valuation-only TRIM signal**, with weighted fair value of **$54.85/share** versus the $210.36 chart quote. The model's base case is $54.18 and bull case $100.24. This is a large valuation gap, but the result is **provisional and low-confidence** because a full bridge from management's adjusted FCF guidance to GAAP-based owner cash flow remains unresolved. The stress output also cannot quantify an operating-margin downside case once normalized terminal FCFF turns negative.

The existing standing decision, `ACCUMULATE_200_EMA_RETEST` at $176–$178, remains stored and is not overwritten by this analysis. Price is now above that entry zone. The user has already trimmed 3 of 12 shares, bringing the recorded position below its 6.75% target. Given the unresolved cash-flow bridge and the now-smaller weight, the provisional DCF signal alone does not support another immediate reduction. The completed trim is a risk-control choice, not a conclusion that the business thesis has failed.

## Business and forward evidence

FY2026 revenue was $3.353B (+25%; +20% excluding Red Canary). ARR was $3.771B (+25%), or $3.630B (+20%) excluding Red Canary's contribution. RPO was $7.365B (+27%), with about 45% current. FY2026 free cash flow was $779M (23% margin, down from 27%); Q4 FCF margin was 7% as spending on platform infrastructure rose. The company reported a 23% non-GAAP operating margin while remaining GAAP operating-loss-making. FY2027 guidance is $3.908B–$3.938B revenue (+16.6%–17.5%), with FCF margin guidance of approximately 23%–23.5%; management reaffirmed its outlook at Investor Day.

AI security is a real growth option, not yet a separately forecastable revenue line. Zscaler said non-seat-based solutions grew over 100% year over year in Q4 and made up about 30% of new and upsell ACV. On the Q4 call, management said the security-for-AI pipeline had grown 75% sequentially; that is a pipeline measure, not booked revenue or ARR. Management's $10B ARR ambition had no dated delivery path in the reviewed materials. The model therefore uses platform growth and a stronger bull-case margin path, but does not add a separate AI revenue premium.

## Annual FCFF valuation

The model starts with FY2026 revenue and management's FY2027 revenue range. The FY2028 revenue mean of $4.58B is only a cross-check because the provider's fiscal-year labels are internally inconsistent. Revenue then grows more slowly through FY2031. The base case assumes GAAP-like operating margins (including SBC) improve from -3% in FY2027 to 10% in FY2031. The bull case reaches 17% in FY2031; the bear case remains near break-even. Scenario weights are 25% bear, 55% base and 20% bull; WACC is 10%, with terminal growth of 2.0%–3.0%.

At 9%–11% WACC and 2%–3% terminal growth, weighted fair value ranges from **$45.24 to $67.15**. The reverse terminal-earnings diagnostic implies roughly 51% annual revenue growth over five years at a 7.9% normalized NOPAT margin and 30x exit P/E to support the current price. This is a deliberately narrow diagnostic, not a second annual FCFF estimate. The local EV/Sales peer check had data for only one peer and is therefore marked insufficient.

The model uses $3.321B cash and short-term investments, subtracts $1.725B of 0% notes due in 2028, and uses management's 173M FY2027 diluted-share assumption. The notes' initial conversion price is $439.52, well above the current quote, so retaining them as debt is reasonable at this date. The model's amortization path includes depreciation, acquired-intangible amortization and deferred contract-acquisition-cost amortization; these totaled about $396M in FY2026 cash-flow addbacks. SBC is already included in the GAAP-like operating margins and is not deducted a second time.

### Cash-flow bridge and model limits

FY2026 SBC expense in the cash-flow statement was $822M. Reported FCF adds this noncash expense back, while this model treats compensation as an owner cost through GAAP operating margins. Under the base assumptions, FY2027 modeled FCFF is approximately **-$178M** despite management's adjusted FCF margin guide of 23%–23.5%. The difference is not a direct apples-to-apples comparison: cash flow also reflects deferred revenue, deferred sales commissions, lease costs, acquisition amortization and working-capital timing. The model's future SBC and working-capital paths are assumptions rather than company guidance. The valuation should be revisited after this bridge is explicitly reconciled; the `NEEDS_REVALUATION` status remains set.

The 10-point operating-margin downside stress cannot be valued as a perpetuity because it makes the bear terminal FCFF negative. The runner now reports that stress as not computable instead of aborting the base valuation. A 25% capex increase gives a weighted value of $47.15; a 15% higher share count gives $47.70.

## Thesis watch points

- Track organic net-new ARR excluding Red Canary, new-logo productivity, and customer retention.
- Check whether the AI-security pipeline becomes recognized ARR and revenue; sequential pipeline growth alone is not enough.
- Reconcile adjusted FCF guidance against SBC, capex, deferred contract-acquisition spend/amortization, deferred revenue and leases.
- Compare actual GAAP operating-margin improvement and diluted-share growth against the scenario paths.
- Revisit the standing decision only after this cash-flow bridge and the next earnings data are assessed.
