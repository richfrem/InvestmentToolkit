## Investment Thesis

### AI Electricity Demand + Nuclear Renaissance = Structural Re-Rating
Vistra owns the largest independent generation fleet in the US (~41GW), including ~4GW of nuclear capacity following the 2024 Energy Harbor acquisition (Perry and Beaver Valley units). Hyperscalers (Microsoft, Google, Amazon, Meta) have committed to 100% carbon-free energy and urgently need 24/7 reliable baseload — something only nuclear and large-scale dispatchable gas can provide. Vistra is one of a handful of companies physically positioned to meet this need at scale.

### The Earnings Volatility Is Cyclical, Not Structural
VST's GAAP net income has swung from -$1.2B (FY2022) to +$2.7B (FY2024) to +$0.9B (TTM). This extreme volatility reflects deregulated power market exposure — VST participates directly in ERCOT (Texas) and PJM spot markets, where prices are set by the marginal generator. The FY2022 loss was driven by the 2022 natural gas price crisis catching VST under-hedged. Since then, the company has improved its hedging program. The TTM decline from FY2024 peak reflects post-cyclical power price normalization, not business deterioration. Analysts project a sharp recovery to ~$9 EPS in FY2026.

### The Share Count Complexity
VST emerged from the Energy Future Holdings bankruptcy in 2016 with ~85.8M warrants outstanding — a legacy capital structure quirk that inflates the apparent diluted share count by 25.4% vs basic. All analyses must use the 424.4M diluted count; using basic shares (338.6M) would overstate EPS by 25%. This is a common trap for retail investors screening VST on basic-share metrics.

### Valuation Is Reasonable, Not Stretched
At $155.28, VST trades at ~17x diluted forward P/E (FY2026 EPS $9.00). The Utilities growth benchmark P/E is 20x; the Energy Renewables median is 35x. If VST achieves the bull case re-rating as an AI infrastructure essential, the multiple expansion from 17x to 28x would add ~65% to the stock on top of the EPS growth. Even in the base case at 20x and $10.89 EPS, the stock is modestly undervalued.

## Key Risks
1. **Commodity Price Collapse** — ERCOT wholesale prices could fall significantly if new wind/solar capacity floods the Texas grid or if an unusually mild weather year reduces scarcity premiums. VST cannot hedge all commodity exposure even with an improved program.
2. **Dilution / Warrant Exercise** — The ~85.8M warrants from the 2016 bankruptcy emergence will eventually be exercised, increasing the share count and diluting EPS. The timing and price level of exercise is uncertain.
3. **Constella Spinoff Execution** — Separating nuclear assets from the broader portfolio is operationally and regulatorily complex. A failed or delayed spinoff defers the multiple expansion expected by the bull case.
4. **AI Demand Timeline** — The market is pricing in significant AI electricity demand growth. If hyperscaler data center buildout is slower than projected (capital discipline, AI productivity disappointment), VST's power price premium evaporates.
5. **ERCOT Market Regulatory Risk** — Texas's deregulated electricity market is periodically subject to reform proposals (post-February 2021 Winter Storm Uri). Regulatory changes that cap prices or require additional reliability reserves could compress margins.

## What to Watch
- **Quarterly EPS recovery**: Does FY2026 track toward consensus $9.00 EPS? This is the most critical validation metric.
- **AI PPA announcements**: Long-term nuclear PPA signings with named hyperscalers are the single biggest share price catalyst.
- **Constella Energy updates**: Any formal announcement or filing regarding the nuclear spinoff moves the bull case probability significantly higher.
