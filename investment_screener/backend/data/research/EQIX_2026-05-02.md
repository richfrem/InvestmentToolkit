## Investment Thesis

Equinix operates arguably the most strategically important real estate portfolio in the world. As the largest retail colocation and interconnection data center provider, it acts as the physical intersection point for the global internet. The explosion of AI is shifting data center requirements from simple storage to highly power-dense, interconnected clusters. Equinix is perfectly positioned to capitalize on this, as evidenced by its consistent 12.1% TTM revenue growth and robust EBITDA margins (driving a 56.59 Rule of 40 score).

However, valuing Equinix requires adjusting for its REIT structure. Because REITs carry massive non-cash depreciation charges, standard P/E ratios are artificially inflated. Equinix currently trades at ~75x trailing earnings. Even when adjusting our DCF model to use elevated exit multiples (40x in the base case) to act as a proxy for the standard Price/AFFO (Adjusted Funds From Operations) metric, the valuation remains stretched. 

Our base case model assumes Equinix will maintain a 10% revenue CAGR and expand net margins to 16% as existing facilities scale. Even with these solid fundamental assumptions and a premium 40x multiple, the intrinsic value sits at $735.41. The current market price of $1085.03 leaves zero margin of safety. Investors are paying a massive premium for the AI narrative, exposing them to significant downside risk if capital costs remain high or cloud migration slows.

## Key Risks
1. Rising interest rates increase the cost of capital for ongoing data center expansion and refinancing.
2. Hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, GCP) increasingly build their own massive data centers, bypassing Equinix.
3. Power availability and grid constraints in key tier-1 markets limit growth.
4. Valuation leaves little room for execution missteps or delayed AI monetization.
