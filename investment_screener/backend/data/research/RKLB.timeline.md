## Investment Thesis

Rocket Lab operates two segments: **Launch Services** (Electron rocket — proven, ~$8M per flight, small-sat focus) and **Space Systems** (spacecraft design, components, manufacturing). The revenue growth story is genuine — TTM revenue grew +63.5% YoY from a 4-year acceleration curve (effectively going from ~$211M to $602M over 3 years). Gross margins are expanding structurally: from 9% in 2022 to 34.4% TTM, demonstrating real operating leverage in the launch and systems businesses.

The **Neutron medium-lift rocket** is the central binary catalyst. If successful, it transitions RKLB from a small-sat niche player (~$8M/flight) into the medium constellation deployment market ($50-80M/flight), directly competing for the government and commercial megaconstellation contracts (NRO, SDA, Kuiper Phase 2, etc.) that represent 10-20x the current TAM. Management has guided for orbital test in 2026, with commercial launches in 2027+. Founder-led (Peter Beck, CEO, ~10% equity stake) with a strong track record — Electron was the first orbital small-rocket from a private company post-SpaceX, and the business has consistently executed.

However, the current valuation prices Neutron success *plus* continued Electron growth *plus* Space Systems scale — simultaneously. At 138x trailing revenue, the stock has pulled dramatically above even the most optimistic analyst targets. The 16 analysts covering RKLB average a $103.91 price target — the stock is currently 38% above that. The risk/reward is entirely asymmetric to the downside at current levels.

For the **Space Data Centers & Defense sub-strategy**: RKLB represents a WATCHLIST entry, not an active position. The thesis is structurally sound (orbital infrastructure for AI-edge compute, government constellation mandates), but the price creates a return-free risk scenario for new money.

## Key Risks

1. **Neutron execution failure**: The rocket has not yet launched. A failed orbital attempt, catastrophic failure, or multi-year delay collapses the bull thesis and leaves RKLB valued as an Electron-only operator (base/bear scenarios) — both of which imply 90%+ downside from current levels.
2. **SpaceX price competition**: SpaceX has demonstrated willingness to undercut competitors. If Falcon 9 prices drop or SpaceX targets the small-sat market more aggressively, Electron pricing power erodes.
3. **Share dilution**: At the current pace of equity financing (~$250-350M/yr), shares outstanding grow 3-4%/yr, directly diluting per-share economics. Neutron is capex-intensive; dilution could accelerate.
4. **Macro risk-off rotation**: At 138x revenue, RKLB is a high-beta (2.31) speculative growth name. Any risk-off rotation, interest rate spike, or tech sector de-rating would be amplified.
5. **Customer concentration**: Government contracts (NASA, NRO, DARPA, ESA) represent a significant portion of backlog — any political or budgetary shift could delay programs.
