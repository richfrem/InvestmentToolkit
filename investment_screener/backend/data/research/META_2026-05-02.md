## Investment Thesis

Meta Platforms operates a near-monopoly in global social networking through its Family of Apps (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp). This unrivaled scale combined with a highly lucrative targeted ad-model generates massive free cash flow ($46.1B TTM). Their financial profile is exceptional, sporting a Rule of 40 score of 85.7, which indicates a rare combination of hypergrowth and extreme profitability.

While the market often penalizes Meta for its heavy reinvestment into Reality Labs and massive AI infrastructure build-out, these investments are building critical future optionality and defensive moats. The AI investments are already yielding tangible returns in the core ad business through better targeting, higher conversion rates, and increased user engagement via tools like Meta AI.

At a trailing P/E of ~22x, Meta is trading at a significant discount to its growth rate and peer group. Our probability-weighted fair value model, which assumes a conservative 22% baseline growth rate and moderate 32% margins, suggests an intrinsic value of $1184.90. This massive 94% margin of safety reflects the market's severe underpricing of Meta's durable cash generation and AI-driven growth vectors.
