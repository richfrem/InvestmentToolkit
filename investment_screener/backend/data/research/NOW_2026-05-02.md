## Investment Thesis

ServiceNow is the undeniable leader in IT Service Management (ITSM) and has successfully expanded its workflow orchestration engine into HR, Customer Service, and Creator workflows. Its financial profile is exceptionally strong: an Rule of 40 score of 44.86, ~22% forward revenue growth, and net margins that have structurally expanded from ~4.5% to over 13% in the last four years. The company is generating $4.5 billion in free cash flow, pointing to a highly efficient operating model.

Despite these robust fundamentals, the stock has experienced a massive selloff (down ~52% YoY). This price action implies a complete breakdown in their growth narrative, which is flatly contradicted by their actual financial performance and forward analyst estimates. While there may be macroeconomic headwinds affecting software multiples generally, the underlying business remains resilient due to the high switching costs and mission-critical nature of its platform.

Our DCF valuation model suggests that even if we assume a conservative 20% growth rate (below historical averages) and an exit P/E of 30x (standard for mature SaaS), the company's fair value is $143.77. This represents a nearly 60% upside from current levels, making it a compelling BUY.
