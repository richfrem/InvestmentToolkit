## Investment Thesis

### The Structural Shift in NAND Cyclicality (LTAs)
Historically, memory hardware stocks have been characterized by brutal boom-and-bust spot price cycles. However, as noted by Bernstein, SanDisk has locked in multiple 3-5 year Long-Term Agreements (LTAs) covering a significant portion of its capacity (including >1/3 of FY2027 shipments). These contracts shift the power balance by establishing range-bound pricing, upfront customer capital prepayments, and minimum price floors (~$0.29/GB). This structurally de-risks SanDisk's downside earnings profile, maintaining strong profitability even during spot market oversupply phases.

### The Agentic AI Swarm Memory Moat
As AI deployments transition from training to global agentic execution, memory requirements are shifting. Agentic AI swarms launch parallel sub-agents that generate substantial loop state caching, continuous trace logging, and vector database embeddings. This creates a non-linear, cost-effective storage demand profile. High-density TLC/QLC enterprise SSDs are the only viable hardware layer to support this tier, placing SanDisk directly at the center of the AI memory bottleneck.

3.  **The Storage Multiplier Effect**: As companies deploy autonomous swarms for coding, software testing, customer service, and market analysis, we project a **10x to 50x multiplier** in enterprise SSD storage capacity per user relative to simple LLM prompt-response frameworks.

## Investment Thesis

SanDisk Corporation was spun off from Western Digital in 2024, creating the largest independent pure-play NAND flash manufacturer and storage solutions company. The company develops and sells SSDs, NAND flash memory, and enterprise storage solutions across consumer electronics, data centers, and AI/ML infrastructure.

The timing of the spinoff was ideal: NAND prices hit multi-decade lows in 2023 (causing $2.14B in losses that year) and are now recovering dramatically. AI/ML model training and inference requires massive NAND storage for model weights, training datasets, and inference caching. Each training run for a frontier model requires petabytes of fast storage — directly benefiting SanDisk.

The revenue trajectory is extraordinary: $6.09B → $6.66B → $7.36B in the first 3 years as an independent company, with analysts projecting $19.4B in the next year. This jump is almost certainly driven by NAND price recovery (ASP expansion) on top of existing volumes — a classic cycle recovery. When NAND prices recover from trough, revenue can double or triple rapidly without proportional volume growth.

The 34.19% TTM net margin reflects peak-cycle NAND pricing. Historical margins were deeply negative during the 2023 trough. Sustainable margins through a full cycle are likely 15-25%. Our base case uses 22% for Y5 — above historical troughs but below current peak.
