- Orders $24.2B, up 88% organically. Revenue $11.1B, up 22% (12% organically).
- Net income $0.6B (5.8% margin); adjusted EBITDA $1.2B (11.3% margin).
- EPS $2.47 against an estimate of $3.22.
- Free cash flow $5.1B; cash $13.1B.
- Backlog $176B (equipment $88B, services $88B); target of $200B in 2027.
- Gas turbines: 116 GW under contract or reserved (53 GW firm backlog, 63 GW slot reservations); floor of 125 GW by year-end 2026. First-half equipment orders priced more than 20% above Q4 2025 levels. Agreements extend into 2031.

- Wind: EBITDA loss widened to $275M from $165M; orders down 40%.

2026 guidance was raised for the second time: revenue $45.5B–$46.5B.

Consensus (33 analysts): FY26 revenue $46.3B, FY27 $52.8B; FY27 EPS $24.78; mean target $1,230, median $1,250.
