## Investment Thesis

Astera Labs has positioned itself as a critical bottleneck supplier in the AI infrastructure buildout. Its PCIe and CXL retimers are essential for connecting the massive arrays of GPUs required for training and inference at scale. This early-mover advantage has translated into explosive financial performance: trailing revenue grew 91.8% to $852.5 million, and the company recently swung from heavy losses to an impressive 25.7% net margin, demonstrating extreme operating leverage inherent in fabless semiconductor models.

However, the valuation leaves zero room for error. At a 166x trailing P/E and a $34.7 billion market capitalization, the market is pricing in perfection. The hypergrowth exception requires us to project a natural deceleration path: while Y1 consensus is nearly 60%, maintaining that scale becomes mathematically prohibitive. Our base case projects a 28% 5-year CAGR, which is still exceptional growth, but when paired with a normalized 30x exit multiple, yields a present value of only $103.87.

The risk/reward profile is highly asymmetric to the downside. Any sign of slowing AI capex by the top hyperscalers, or the introduction of in-house developed connectivity chips by Amazon/Microsoft/Google, could trigger a catastrophic multiple contraction. At a weighted fair value of $112.46, the stock is deeply overvalued based on discounted future cash flows.
