Core Scientific (CORZ) has ridden the post-bankruptcy AI/HPC hype wave to a $6.4B valuation. My weighted fair value of $5.71 indicates massive downside risk.

## Investment Thesis
CORZ is another crypto-miner executing an HPC/AI data center pivot, but the economic reality is inescapable.

1. **The Capex Treadmill**: Building gigawatt data centers requires billions in capex. Last year, CORZ burned $460M in FCF. Funding the pipeline at a $6.4B market cap guarantees significant equity dilution.
2. **Dilution Reality**: The company's massive restructuring and ongoing capex needs mean the share count will inevitably expand. I model a 25% dilution in the base case, suppressing per-share returns even if the company scales top-line revenue effectively.
3. **Priced for Perfection**: At $20.35 per share, the market assumes the AI pivot is fully funded and already generating software-like margins. The current price offers zero margin of safety against execution delays or capital market freezes.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $4.53 (45% Prob)
CORZ achieves a 30% revenue CAGR over 5 years as its AI hosting division scales, reaching $1.2B in revenue. Net margins eventually stabilize at 12%. However, the massive capital required to build out the sites forces a 25% dilution of the equity base. The market awards a 20x P/E for a stabilizing infrastructure business.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $0.39 (35% Prob)
AI hosting contracts get delayed or capital markets dry up. Growth stutters at 10% annually. Margins remain poor at 5%. The massive ongoing cash burn forces a 50% dilution at depressed share prices. The market de-rates the stock to a 15x P/E.

Flawless execution. CORZ signs massive, high-margin HPC contracts (like major CoreWeave deals). Growth averages 40% for 5 years, with net margins reaching 20%. Dilution is kept to 10% due to lucrative upfront tenant financing. The market awards a 25x P/E.
