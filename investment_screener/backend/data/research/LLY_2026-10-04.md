## Summary

Lilly is close to fairly valued. Fair value is $982.68 at a 10% discount rate (14.0% below the price), $1,052.51 at 8.5%, and $1,094.72 at the company's own 7.65% computed WACC (4.2% below). There is no margin of safety at this price. As a watchlist name it would need a pullback toward $900 to offer a 15% discount to the 8.5% fair value.

Q3 2026 results are due on 2026-10-29, and an investment community meeting follows on 2026-12-07.

## Operating picture

Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-05):

- Revenue $22.97B, up 48%. Volume rose 60%; realized prices fell 13%.
- Mounjaro $9.9B (up 91%); Zepbound $4.93B (up 46%). Together $14.87B, 65% of sales.
- US revenue $14.4B (up 33%); international $8.6B (up 80%), with prices abroad down 36%, mostly from Mounjaro joining China's reimbursement list.
- EPS $7.94 reported, $8.38 non-GAAP, both after $3.03 a share of acquired R&D charges.

2026 guidance after Q2: revenue $85B–$87B (raised from $82B–$85B); non-GAAP EPS $35.50–$36.50 (top end lowered by $0.50 because the acquired R&D charges offset stronger operations).

Pipeline: orforglipron (Foundayo), an oral GLP-1, is FDA-approved for obesity. The retatrutide Phase 3 package is complete, with a filing planned for Q1 2027.

Consensus (29 analysts): FY26 revenue $88.5B, FY27 $102.1B; FY26 EPS $36.95, FY27 $47.59; mean target $1,329, median $1,385.

## What would change this view

- Orforglipron sales scaling without diluting margins.
- Price erosion slowing.
- A pullback toward $900.

## Key risks

- Price erosion in GLP-1s and drug-pricing policy.
- Acquired R&D charges recurring.
