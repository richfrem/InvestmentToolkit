## Investment Thesis
HUT is an infrastructure trap wrapped in an AI narrative.

1. **Valuation Decoupled from Reality**: A market cap of $8.6B on $15M of revenue implies a 574x trailing sales multiple. The market is pricing HUT as if it is already a dominant, highly profitable hyperscaler, entirely ignoring the immense execution risk and capital required to get there.
2. **The FCF Black Hole**: HUT burned -$787M in free cash flow over the trailing twelve months. Building gigawatt-scale data centers is one of the most capital-intensive businesses in the world. 
3. **Punishing Dilution**: Because operations provide negative cash flow, HUT must tap the capital markets to fund its capex. I am modeling a 50% increase in the share count in the base case just to keep the lights on and fund the build-out. This dilution mathematically crushes per-share value.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $8.78 (45% Prob)
HUT manages to execute its AI hosting pivot. Revenue grows at a blistering 80% CAGR for 5 years. Net margins eventually stabilize at 10%. However, the massive capital required forces a 50% dilution of the equity base. The market awards a 15x P/E. Due to the massive dilution and the low base revenue, the Year 5 share price is heavily suppressed.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $1.25 (35% Prob)
The AI pivot stumbles due to intense hyperscaler competition or inability to secure power. Growth slows to 30%. Margins remain near 0%. The $700M+ cash burn forces a staggering 80% dilution at depressed equity prices. Exit P/E is 10. The equity collapses.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $23.82 (20% Prob)
Miraculous execution. HUT secures massive, high-margin HPC contracts. Growth averages 120% for 5 years, with net margins reaching 20%. Dilution is kept to 30% due to lucrative upfront tenant financing. The market awards a 25x P/E. Even in this pristine scenario, the stock is heavily overvalued today.
