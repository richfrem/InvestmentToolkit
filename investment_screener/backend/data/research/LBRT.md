## Investment Thesis

Liberty Oilfield Services provides hydraulic fracturing and completion services to oil & gas E&P companies, primarily in the Permian Basin and other US shale formations. The company rode the 2022 Russia/Ukraine energy crisis to record revenue ($4.75B) and margins (11.7%), but every year since has seen fundamental deterioration.

The core problem is that oilfield services is a commodity business. LBRT competes with ProPetro (PUMP), NexTier, Halliburton, Schlumberger, and dozens of smaller operators on price. When E&P companies cut drilling budgets (as they have since 2023), service companies engage in price competition that destroys margins. The current 3.7% TTM margin represents exactly this dynamic — revenue is only slightly declining (+4.5% YoY) but margins have been cut from 11.7% to 3.7% by pricing pressure.

The forward PE of 92x is the clearest SELL signal. Paying 92 years of current earnings for a commodity service company facing margin deterioration is not a rational investment. Analyst consensus of $32.77 target (0.3% above current) with a "Buy" rating is internally contradictory — this appears to be a stale rating that has not been updated since the business deterioration accelerated.

Even the bull case — oil price recovery to $90+, E&P capex surge, margin restoration to FY2022 peak — gives $39.64 fair value, only 21% above current price. Asymmetric risk/reward: -61% downside in base case, only +21% upside in optimistic bull. SELL.
