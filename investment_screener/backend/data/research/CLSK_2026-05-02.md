## TL;DR
CleanSpark (CLSK) is a major Bitcoin miner that has successfully scaled its top-line to $766M. However, it remains trapped in the brutal capex cycle of cryptocurrency mining, burning $1.02B in free cash flow over the last year. Funding this massive cash burn requires continuous equity dilution. While Bitcoin prices heavily dictate the bull/bear outcomes, the fundamental business model is structurally disadvantaged by hardware depreciation and energy intensity. The weighted PV of $6.09 indicates significant downside.

## Investment Thesis
CLSK is a high-beta proxy for Bitcoin, but with terrible capital economics.

1. **The Capex Treadmill**: CLSK burned $1.02B in FCF over the last year. Compute infrastructure is a hyper-depreciating asset. By the time a mining rig pays for itself, it is obsolete and requires replacement to maintain network hash rate share.
2. **Inevitable Dilution**: Because the business does not organically fund its own growth or hardware refresh cycles, it must constantly tap the capital markets. Share counts inevitably explode over time in this sector.
3. **No Structural Moat**: Mining is a pure commodity service. They have no pricing power against the network and are at the mercy of wholesale hardware providers for supply.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $5.43 (45% Prob)
The company maintains a modest 15% growth rate. Crucially, the company dilutes the share base by 25% over 5 years to keep the lights on and refresh hardware. The market awards a low 15x P/E, recognizing it as a low-quality commodity provider dependent on Bitcoin price stability.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $1.25 (35% Prob)
Compute margins collapse due to a crypto winter. Severe dilution (40%) is required to avoid bankruptcy. The equity is severely impaired.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $16.03 (20% Prob)
An extended crypto supercycle drives massive revenue growth (30%) and high net margins (25%). Dilution is contained to 10% because organic cash flows finally cover the capex requirements. Only under these pristine conditions does the stock beat its current $12.17 valuation.
