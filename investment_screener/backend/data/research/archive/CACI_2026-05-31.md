## Investment Thesis
CACI International Inc has built a robust competitive moat based on deep government incumbency, long-term multi-year contracts, and a highly cleared workforce (over 70% of employees hold active security clearances). These characteristics create significant switching costs for federal civilian, intelligence, and defense agencies. CACI acts as a trusted partner, executing critical missions across national security domains.

Furthermore, CACI is executing a successful strategic shift from traditional low-margin IT services and staff augmentation to proprietary, high-value mission technology. Its solutions in free-space optical communications (photonics), offensive and defensive cyber operations, electronic warfare, and space-based intelligence fusion command higher margins and enjoy rapid volume growth as geopolitical tensions escalate. This is reflected in its recent EBITDA margins approaching 11.12%.

However, valuation is the primary constraint. Trading at 22.8x trailing earnings, CACI is valued at a steep premium relative to its historical multiples and its sector median multiple of 18.0x. While the defense backlog provides defensive cash flows, government spending uncertainties, potential continuing resolutions (CRs), and labor cost pressures for cleared technical personnel present risks to profit expansion. We believe the current stock price has overshot the fundamental intrinsic value.

## Key Risks
1. **Government Budget Uncertainty**: Continuing resolutions (CRs) or debt-ceiling standoffs could freeze government spending, delaying new contract awards or contract funding.
2. **Labor Shortage & Cost Inflation**: A critical shortage of qualified, security-cleared technical professionals could drive labor cost inflation, impacting margins on fixed-price contracts.
3. **Defense Contracting Competition**: Increased bidding competition from diversified primes (Leidos, Booz Allen, SAIC, General Dynamics) could erode pricing power.
4. **Transition Execution**: Failure to scale the high-value mission technology products as rapidly as expected could lead to lower-than-projected net margins.
