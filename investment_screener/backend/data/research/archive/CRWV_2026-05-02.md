## Investment Thesis

CoreWeave is the defining GPU-cloud pure-play of the GenAI era. The company went from $16M revenue in 2022 to $5.1B TTM — a trajectory with no real comparable in cloud infrastructure history. The core question is not whether this growth is real (it clearly is), but whether CoreWeave can sustain it long enough to become a durable business before hyperscalers commoditize the market.

The gross margin story is compelling. At 71.7%, CoreWeave is demonstrating that GPU cloud access commands genuine pricing power — similar to how early AWS could charge premium prices before Azure/GCP competition drove margins down. The declining trend (74.3% FY2024 → 71.7% TTM) is worth watching, but 71.7% remains exceptional for infrastructure.

The FCF story is alarming. The company is burning -$7.25B TTM — worsening year-over-year (-$1.1B → -$5.95B → -$7.25B). This is not a sign of financial distress (the debt is asset-backed by the GPU fleet), but it creates a clear dependency: the AI capex supercycle must continue for CoreWeave to remain solvent. Any significant slowdown in AI training demand would trigger a debt-coverage crisis.

The Weights & Biases acquisition is strategically important. If CoreWeave transitions from pure GPU rental to a platform with model-development tooling, data gravity, and Kubernetes-native integrations, the switching costs rise dramatically. This is the critical variable separating the base and bull cases.

At $119/share vs our fair value of $162.55, there is meaningful upside in the base case. The 30% weight on the bear case means this is not a safe hold — position sizing discipline is essential.

## Key Risks

1. **FCF deterioration**: FCF is worsening (-$7.25B TTM vs -$5.95B FY2024). The GPU buildout is not yet self-funding. Any lender covenant tightening or debt refinancing at higher rates could be a catalyst for distress.

2. **Hyperscaler competition**: AWS EC2 P5 and Google A3 already compete on GPU access. Microsoft — CoreWeave's largest customer — is simultaneously building its own GPU capacity. The customer/competitor dynamic creates existential risk.

3. **Customer concentration**: Microsoft is estimated to represent ~60% of CoreWeave revenue. Loss or significant reduction of this contract would be immediately catastrophic.

4. **Gross margin trend**: Declining from 74.3% → 71.7% — early sign of pricing pressure. If this reaches 65%, the base case profitability path becomes very challenging.

5. **Equity dilution**: 442M shares now vs likely 500M+ after option vesting. Pre-IPO option pool and RSU grants are still vesting; full diluted share count may be materially higher than current reported figure.

## What To Watch

- **Quarterly gross margin**: Every quarter below 70% is a bear case signal
- **New customer diversification**: Announcements of non-Microsoft enterprise anchors are bull case signals
- **FCF trajectory**: Watch for any quarter with FCF improvement (less negative) — inflection is the key bull thesis enabler
- **W&B platform metrics**: Platform revenue as % of total revenue; if growing, validates the anti-commodity thesis
- **Hyperscaler GPU pricing**: AWS/GCP/Azure GPU instance pricing trends are the best real-time indicator of CoreWeave's competitive position
