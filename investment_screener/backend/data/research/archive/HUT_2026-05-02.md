## Investment Thesis

Hut 8 Corp is a Bitcoin mining company. That description alone should anchor the valuation analysis: the company's revenues, margins, and earnings are almost entirely driven by the price of Bitcoin and the amount of BTC it can mine, not by traditional business fundamentals. At $76.98 per share with a $8.66B market cap against $15M in TTM GAAP revenue, the implied price-to-sales ratio of 575x is one of the most extreme in any public market.

The $15M TTM revenue reflects the post-halving transitional period. Bitcoin underwent its fourth halving in April 2024, cutting block rewards from 6.25 to 3.125 BTC per block. This mechanically halved mining revenue. Analysts expect a recovery to $344M in Y1 and $543M in Y2 as BTC price appreciation and operational scale offset the halving impact. Even at $344M revenue, the challenge is clear: at 3% GAAP net margin, NI is only $10M, giving $0.09 EPS. A 15x PE on $0.09 EPS = $1.35/share. The market is pricing $76.98.

The fundamental driver of the current valuation is the BTC spot price and the expectation of continued appreciation. HUT 8 holds a significant BTC treasury on its balance sheet — this BTC marked-to-market at current prices could theoretically justify a higher NAV per share than the DCF. But even this analysis is challenging: HUT 8 continues to burn massive cash (FCF -$787M TTM) on new mining infrastructure, effectively diluting BTC NAV with ongoing capital destruction.

The analyst "Strong Buy" at $84.50 reflects BTC price trajectory modeling, not fundamental earnings analysis. EPS forecasts confirm this: Y1 -$1.76, Y2 -$3.17 — analysts expect the company to LOSE MORE MONEY in each of the next two years, yet maintain a Strong Buy rating because they're pricing BTC at $100K+.

## Key Risks to the SELL Thesis (Bull Arguments)

1. **BTC at $200K+** — If Bitcoin achieves widespread institutional adoption as a global reserve asset, HUT 8's mining operations and BTC treasury could justify current prices. This is a macro thesis, not a company-specific fundamental.
2. **AI/HPC hosting transformation** — HUT 8 has announced AI/HPC hosting ambitions using its data center infrastructure. If this becomes a meaningful revenue stream with tech-company margins, the DCF could improve dramatically.
3. **BTC halving recovery** — The $15M TTM is an anomalously low base. Analyst $344M Y1 estimate reflects normalization. The 575x P/S is misleading when using the trough TTM.

---

## Key Risks to the Bull Thesis (Bear Arguments)

1. **FCF deeply negative** — FCF was -$787M TTM. The company burns $1B+ per year in capital. At this rate, without BTC price appreciation, the equity is consumed by capital destruction.
2. **Analyst EPS forecasts are negative** — Y1 -$1.76, Y2 -$3.17. Getting worse. Strong Buy rating is purely a BTC price bet.
3. **No durable competitive advantage** — Any company can buy mining equipment. Energy costs, ASICs procurement, and regulatory risk are all commodity/external factors that HUT 8 does not control.
4. **Piotroski 2/9** — Cash flow negative, ROA negative, ROA not improving. Financial health is deteriorating.
