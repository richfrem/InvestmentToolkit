## Investment Thesis

CACI is not a typical government IT contractor. Founded in 1962 and headquartered in Reston, VA, it has spent the past decade systematically upgrading its work from commodity IT services toward classified national security technology: cyber warfare operations, electronic warfare, spectrum superiority (EW/ISR), free-space optical communications (photonics), and AI-enabled intelligence fusion.

The $28B+ backlog — approximately 3.2x TTM revenue — is the most important number in the CACI story. Government contracts are rarely annulled; they are extended, re-competed, and won again by incumbents who have the clearances and institutional knowledge. This backlog provides multi-year revenue visibility that commercial technology companies simply cannot replicate.

The cleared workforce is CACI's deepest moat. More than 70% of employees hold Top Secret/SCI clearances. These take 18-36 months to obtain and require continued employment to maintain. No competitor can simply hire away CACI's cleared workforce — the market for cleared personnel is tight, and CACI's 60-year institutional relationships in the IC and DoD give it persistent access to programs that newer entrants cannot even bid on.

The risk in 2026 is DOGE. The federal budget environment is the most uncertain it has been since the 2011 Budget Control Act sequestration. CACI's stock has reflected this anxiety — down 23% in three months — but the market appears to be applying a blanket defense IT discount that doesn't differentiate between commodity IT outsourcing (genuinely vulnerable) and classified national security programs (typically protected). CACI's revenue mix skews heavily toward the latter.

At $466 the stock trades at 14.9x forward P/E against a sector median of ~18x. That discount is warranted but mostly already reflects the budget risk. The question for investors is whether the discount goes further (DOGE actually cuts IC programs) or begins to close as clarity emerges. My base case says fair value is $436 — implying the current price is modestly stretched.

## Key Risks

1. **DOGE/federal budget sequestration** — The most significant near-term risk. A broad federal spending freeze or continuing resolution that delays contract awards could compress revenue growth toward the bear case. Watch: FY2027 NDAA passage and defense appropriations timeline.

2. **Single-customer concentration** — ~95% of revenue from US federal government. Any administration pivot on defense spending directly impacts CACI's top line. No commercial offset exists.

3. **Contract re-compete risk** — Government contracts re-compete every 5-7 years. CACI's win rate is historically high (~90%), but a competitor winning one of the top-10 IDIQ vehicles would be material. Watch: any LPTA (lowest price technically acceptable) shift in DoD procurement policy.

4. **Cleared workforce retention** — With the cleared labor market tight, poaching risk from defense primes (L3Harris, Leidos, SAIC) is real. Labor costs are rising; fixed-price contract margins are most exposed.

5. **Photonics commercialization risk** — The bull case relies on CACI's free-space optical communications technology winning large Space Force programs. This is optionality, not a certainty. Technology readiness level (TRL) and program of record status are key watchpoints.
