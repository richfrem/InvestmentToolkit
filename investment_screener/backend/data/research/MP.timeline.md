# MP Materials Corp. (NYSE: MP) — Fundamental Research & Valuation Profile

## Executive Summary
MP Materials is the largest producer of refined rare earth materials in the Western Hemisphere, operating the tier-1 Mountain Pass mine in California. The company is actively executing a vertical integration pivot: transitioning from exporting raw bastnäsite concentrate (Stage 1) to on-site Neodymium-Praseodymium (NdPr) refining (Stage 2) and full-scale NdFeB permanent magnet manufacturing in Fort Worth, Texas (Stage 3). 

## Structural Growth Drivers
1. **Robotics & Physical AI Actuation**: Permanent NdFeB magnets represent ~30% of rare earth consumption by volume and >90% by value. Each humanoid robot requires 40+ precision permanent magnet brushless motors.
2. **Defense Critical Onshoring**: MP maintains direct commercial supply and capability expansion contracts with the US Department of Defense to establish an end-to-end domestic supply chain for defense weapon systems (F-35, missile guidance, defense radar).
3. **Automotive & Clean Energy**: Long-term supply agreements with General Motors and Apple for domestic magnetics and recycled rare earth feedstock.
