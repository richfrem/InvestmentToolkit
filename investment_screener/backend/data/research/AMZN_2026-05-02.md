## Investment Thesis

Amazon is undergoing a profound structural shift in its profitability profile. Historically known for razor-thin margins as it built out its retail fulfillment network, the company is now reaping the rewards of two high-margin juggernauts: Amazon Web Services (AWS) and its rapidly growing advertising business. 

This mix shift is clearly visible in the financials. Over the last four years, net margins have trended aggressively upwards: -0.5% -> 5.3% -> 9.3% -> 10.8% TTM. Operating cash flow is immense, and free cash flow has rebounded to nearly $33 billion. A perfect Piotroski F-Score of 9 confirms the fundamental strength and improving capital efficiency across the business.

While the trailing P/E of 32x might screen as expensive for a traditional retailer, Amazon must be valued as a tech platform. As AWS scales to capture the impending wave of enterprise AI training and inference workloads, operating leverage should push blended margins comfortably into the mid-teens. Even applying a conservative 26x exit multiple on these expanding earnings yields significant upside.
