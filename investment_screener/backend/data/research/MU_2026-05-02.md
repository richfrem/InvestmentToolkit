## Investment Thesis

Micron Technology has structurally transitioned from a cyclical provider of commoditized PC and smartphone memory into a critical bottleneck supplier for the global AI infrastructure buildout. High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM), essential for modern AI GPUs (like Nvidia's Hopper and Blackwell architectures), is extremely difficult to manufacture, supply-constrained, and carries massively higher gross margins than traditional DRAM.

This dynamic has triggered an unprecedented hypergrowth phase for Micron. TTM revenue is up 196%, and the company has violently swung from a -37.5% net margin in the previous cyclical trough back to a highly profitable 22.8% margin. The Rule of 40 score of nearly 246 illustrates the sheer velocity of this inflection.

Despite this, the market remains highly skeptical, pricing MU at a mere 25.6x trailing P/E and a minuscule 5.3x forward P/E. Investors are seemingly pricing in an immediate, severe cyclical crash akin to historical memory cycles. However, the secular demand from hyperscalers building out AGI infrastructure suggests this cycle will be "higher for longer." Our DCF model conservatively forces a rapid deceleration of the 191% Y1 growth estimate down to a 50% 5-year CAGR. Even with this penalty and a normalized 25x exit multiple, the intrinsic value sits at $1849.65. This presents one of the most asymmetric risk/reward profiles in the large-cap tech sector.
