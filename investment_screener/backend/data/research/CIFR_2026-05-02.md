Cipher Digital (CIFR) is trading at a premium $6.9B valuation (30x trailing sales) as it aggressively pivots from Bitcoin mining to High-Performance Computing (HPC) data centers. ## Investment Thesis
Like its peers (APLD, BTDR), CIFR is running the infrastructure capex treadmill. 

1. **The Capex Black Hole**: Building HPC data centers requires massive upfront investment in land, power, and cooling. CIFR burned $3 for every $1 of revenue generated last year. This structural reality means they are highly dependent on external capital.
2. **Dilution Risk**: To fund the development pipeline without taking on crippling debt, CIFR will have to heavily dilute current shareholders. I am modeling a 40% increase in the share count over the next 5 years just to sustain the base case growth.
3. **Priced for Perfection**: At 30x trailing sales, the market is assuming CIFR will easily secure tier-1 hyperscaler contracts, face no delays in grid interconnection, and execute complex construction flawlessly. There is zero margin of safety at $17.04.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $5.92 (45% Prob)
CIFR executes its HPC pivot successfully. Revenue compounds at a 55% CAGR over 5 years. Net margins eventually reach 12%. However, the massive capital required to build out the sites forces a 40% dilution of the equity base. The market awards a 20x P/E for a stabilizing infrastructure business.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $1.15 (35% Prob)
HPC hosting becomes oversupplied or hyperscalers prefer to build their own sites. Growth slows to 25%. Margins compress to 5%. The massive ongoing cash burn forces a 60% dilution at depressed share prices. The market de-rates the stock to a 15x P/E.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $14.80 (20% Prob)
Flawless execution. CIFR signs massive, high-margin, long-term HPC contracts with major hyperscalers. Growth averages 85% for 5 years, with net margins reaching 20%. Dilution is kept to a more manageable 20% due to lucrative upfront tenant financing. The market awards a 30x P/E.
