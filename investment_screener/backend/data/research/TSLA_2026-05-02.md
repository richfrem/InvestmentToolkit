## Investment Thesis

Tesla has cemented its position as the premier western electric vehicle manufacturer, but its core automotive business is currently facing severe margin pressure. TTM net margins have collapsed from historical highs near 15.5% down to just 4.0% as the company engaged in aggressive price wars to defend market share against formidable Chinese competitors like BYD. 

Despite this fundamental deterioration, the stock is trading at a breathtaking 358x trailing earnings. The market is entirely ignoring the current reality of the automotive market and instead valuing Tesla as an AI and robotics monopoly, heavily discounting future cash flows from currently unproven initiatives like the Robotaxi network (Cybercab) and Optimus humanoid robots. 

While Tesla's energy storage business and Supercharger network present durable, high-margin revenue streams, they are not large enough to justify a $1.47 trillion market cap on their own. Even applying a very generous 45x exit multiple in our base case—which assumes they successfully re-accelerate growth to 12% and recover margins to 10% through FSD software sales—the resulting fair value is $191.79. The current price of $390.82 offers zero margin of safety and immense multiple contraction risk if the AI narratives are delayed or fail to materialize.
