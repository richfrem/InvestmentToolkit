## Investment Thesis

### The GAAP/Economic Margin Disconnect
Zscaler's GAAP income statement tells a misleading story. Net income of -$41.5M and net margin of -1.55% TTM are distorted by ~$768M in stock-based compensation (~29% of revenue). Strip SBC out and ZS generates $726.7M in free cash flow — a 27.2% FCF margin that has been stable to improving across four fiscal years (21.2% → 20.6% → 27.0% → 27.2%). The business is highly profitable by every economic measure except the one automated screeners use. For valuation purposes, FCF/non-GAAP proxy margins are the appropriate anchor, consistent with how the market prices ZS (forward P/E of 30.5x references $4.02 non-GAAP EPS, not GAAP).

### Zero Trust Is Not Optional — ZS Is the Architecture Leader
The hybrid work and multi-cloud transition has permanently broken perimeter-based security. VPNs route traffic through corporate datacenters; in a world where users work from everywhere and apps live in AWS/Azure/SaaS platforms, the bottleneck kills performance and creates a massive attack surface. Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) is the architectural replacement: verify identity, inspect every packet inline, apply least-privilege access regardless of location.

ZS is the largest purpose-built ZTNA provider. Its Zero Trust Exchange processes 400B+ daily transactions across 150+ data centers — a scale that generates proprietary threat intelligence improving with each additional customer (data network effect). Competitors building ZTNA on top of traditional firewall architectures (Fortinet, Check Point) face inherent latency and architecture disadvantages. On-premise vendors cannot economically replicate ZS's inline inspection at cloud scale.

### Switching Costs Create Durable Lock-In
ZIA (internet access security) and ZPA (private application access) are woven into enterprise network architecture at the routing layer. Replacing ZS requires redesigning how traffic flows across the entire organization — not swapping a software license. This is a multi-year, multi-million-dollar project that most IT teams undertake once a decade. This structural switching cost is the primary moat and drives net revenue retention rates consistently above 125%.

### Regulatory Tailwinds Provide Unique Pipeline Visibility
OMB Memorandum M-22-09 ("Moving the US Government Toward Zero Trust") mandates Zero Trust architecture adoption across all federal agencies. ZS is a named reference architecture in CISA's Zero Trust Maturity Model. This creates a multi-year government spending pipeline with mandatory compliance deadlines — the public sector is the most durable, least cyclical growth vector in enterprise software.

### Valuation Dislocation After -38% Drawdown
The stock has fallen from ~$230 to $139.81 over the past year (and -36.6% YTD) as growth decelerated from 34% to 23% and Rule of 40 score slipped to 29.94. The market has repriced ZS from a premium growth SaaS to a more average multiple. We believe this re-rating has overshot — at 8.4x revenue, ZS is priced for continued growth deceleration toward ~15% CAGR, while analyst consensus projects 22% over Y1/Y2. Our base DCF fair value of $202 (base case only) already exceeds the current price by 44%; the probability-weighted value of $233 offers 67% upside.

## Key Risks

1. **Microsoft bundling (highest probability)**: Microsoft's 365 enterprise relationships give it unmatched distribution to bundle Entra ID + Defender as a "good enough" ZTNA substitute at no incremental cost. This primarily threatens ZS's new logo acquisition in the mid-market where procurement is price-sensitive.

2. **PANW platformization expansion**: Palo Alto's strategy of giving away platform access to win long-term lock-in is directly competitive at the enterprise level. If PANW expands free offerings to ZPA-equivalent functionality, it could pressure ZS's net revenue retention rate — the most important KPI to watch.

3. **SBC compression slower than expected**: The entire thesis depends on SBC/revenue falling from 29% toward 11-15% as revenue scales. If ZS continues to grant equity at current rates (to retain talent in a competitive market), GAAP profitability and P/E re-rating never materialise.

4. **Growth deceleration below consensus**: The market has already de-rated ZS once for missing growth expectations. If Y1 growth comes in below 20% (vs analyst consensus 24.1%), the stock likely re-tests lows regardless of FCF quality.

5. **Valuation re-rating risk**: At 30.5x forward non-GAAP P/E and negative GAAP earnings, ZS is still priced for growth. A broader SaaS multiple compression (rising rates, risk-off environment) could push the stock below our bear fair value even without fundamental deterioration.

## What to Watch

- **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: Must stay above 120% for the base thesis. Slipping below 115% signals competitive displacement.
- **Non-GAAP operating margin trend**: Should expand 200-300bp per year. Flat or declining = SBC problem.
- **Federal/public sector revenue mix**: Growing public sector from ~10% to 15%+ revenue validates the OMB catalyst.
- **New logo growth vs installed base expansion**: Slowing new logos + accelerating upsell = healthy; both slowing = structural problem.
- **PANW Q-results for ZTNA**: Watch for any commentary on ZPA-competitive wins or losses during PANW earnings.
