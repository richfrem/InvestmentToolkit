## Operating picture

Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-03):

- Casgevy sales $76M, up 78% from Q1 and 151% year on year. Q1 2026 was $43M; full-year 2025 was $116M. Sales are booked by partner Vertex; CRISPR shares 40% of the programme economics.
- Net loss $91.2M, narrower than a year earlier.
- Cash and marketable securities about $2.36B, helped by a March 2026 convertible note offering that netted $585.4M.
- FDA label expanded to patients aged 2 and up with sickle cell disease or beta thalassemia, about 5,500 additional patients. Casgevy is approved in 39 countries; German reimbursement was agreed in May.
- Pipeline: CTX340 (refractory hypertension) and CTX460 (alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency) entered Phase 1; CTX213 (type 1 diabetes) is preclinical.

The company gives no financial guidance. Consensus (18 analysts): FY26 revenue $41M, FY27 $157M with a range of $2M to $509M; mean target $87.56, range $44 to $291.

## Limitations

- The base revenue is a forward consensus figure, not trailing revenue ($11M), which is not representative.
- Residual cash per scenario is an estimate.
- A risk-adjusted net present value of the pipeline is the appropriate method for this company and was not performed.

## What would change this view

- Positive in vivo cardiovascular data.
- Casgevy quarterly sales above $150M.
- A partnership or milestone that funds the pipeline without dilution.
