## Investment Thesis
RIOT's core business model is a continuous wealth-destruction machine for shareholders.

1. **The Capex Treadmill**: In the trailing twelve months, they generated $647M in revenue but burned $1.14B in free cash flow. This is the definition of the crypto mining 'capex treadmill', where computing hardware becomes obsolete almost as fast as it is deployed. 
2. **Inevitable Dilution**: Because operations generate deeply negative cash flows, the company must constantly tap the equity markets to fund operations and hardware refreshes. I model a massive 35% dilution in the base case just for them to survive. This fundamentally destroys per-share value over time.
3. **No Margin of Safety**: At a $7.01B valuation, the market is pricing in an eternal Bitcoin supercycle. If Bitcoin prices stall or drop, RIOT's negative margins will accelerate the cash burn, forcing even more painful dilution.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $1.70 (45% Prob)
Bitcoin prices moderately increase over time. RIOT manages a 15% revenue CAGR. However, the relentless hardware refresh cycle limits net margins to 5%. To fund the -$1B+ annual cash burn, management issues massive amounts of equity (35% dilution). The market awards a 15x Exit P/E. Due to the extreme dilution, per-share value collapses.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $0.15 (35% Prob)
A prolonged crypto winter sets in. Hash rates drop, and RIOT's revenue shrinks by 5% annually. Net margins sit at 0%. Desperate for cash to avoid bankruptcy, the company floods the market with shares (60% dilution) at rock-bottom prices. The equity is virtually wiped out.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $16.80 (20% Prob)
A massive, sustained Bitcoin bull run. RIOT's revenue compounds at 30% for 5 years. High Bitcoin prices finally allow them to reach 15% net margins and organically fund *some* of their capex, limiting dilution to 15%. Even in this incredibly optimistic scenario, the calculated present value is lower than today's $18.50 share price.
