## Investment Thesis

Celsius Holdings has been one of the most explosive growth stories in the consumer packaged goods sector, successfully disrupting the energy drink duopoly of Monster and Red Bull by marketing itself as a "healthy" functional beverage. The transition to the PepsiCo distribution network turbocharged its reach, driving trailing twelve-month revenue up a staggering 117% to $2.5 billion. Its Rule of 40 score stands at an exceptional 125.29.

However, the financial profile remains highly volatile. The company's net margins have swung wildly (-28.6% in 2022 to 17.2% in 2023, settling back down to 4.3% TTM). This volatility suggests that massive marketing and promotional spend is required to maintain this hypergrowth and secure premium shelf space. More alarmingly, despite the strong trailing growth, the stock has collapsed by over 34% in the last 3 months. The market is clearly anticipating a severe deceleration in growth and questioning the long-term steady-state margin profile.

At a trailing P/E of ~135x and a forward P/E of ~16.6x, the valuation leaves very little room for error. Even giving Celsius the benefit of the doubt with a hypergrowth deceleration model—assuming a 15% 5-year CAGR and normalizing margins to 12%—our base case fair value sits at $21.93. The current price of $33.73 represents a -35% margin of safety. Investors are taking on significant execution and competitive risk at current levels.
