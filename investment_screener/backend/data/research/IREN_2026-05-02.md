IREN (formerly Iris Energy) is trading at a staggering $15.1B valuation, fueled by the aggressive expansion of its dual Bitcoin/HPC data center model. To fund this arms race, current shareholders will face significant equity dilution. The current valuation is stretched but supported by aggressive analyst targets, making the stock a HOLD.

## Investment Thesis
IREN is executing a high-stakes pivot from pure crypto mining into AI infrastructure hosting.

1. **The Capex Treadmill**: Building gigawatt-scale data centers requires billions in capital expenditure. Over the last twelve months, IREN burned -$1.12B in free cash flow. This structural reality means they are highly dependent on external capital.
2. **Dilution Risk**: To fund the development pipeline without taking on crippling debt, IREN will have to dilute current shareholders. I am modeling a 35% increase in the share count in the base case to fund the build-out. 
3. **HPC Optionality**: Unlike pure Bitcoin miners, IREN is actively converting its power capacity into high-margin AI/HPC hosting. Analysts forecast revenue will skyrocket to nearly $2.8B by 2027. If they execute flawlessly on this transition, the resulting cash flows can overpower the dilution effect.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $31.95 (45% Prob)
IREN successfully executes its HPC pivot. Revenue compounds at a 65% CAGR over 5 years. Net margins stabilize at a healthy 15%. However, the massive capital required to build out the sites forces a 35% dilution of the equity base. The market awards a 25x P/E for a stabilizing infrastructure business. The resulting present value of $31.95 suggests the stock is currently overvalued in the base case.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $1.63 (35% Prob)
HPC hosting becomes oversupplied, and the company remains heavily exposed to Bitcoin price volatility. Growth slows to 30%. Margins compress to 5%. The massive ongoing cash burn forces a 60% dilution at depressed share prices. The market de-rates the stock to a 15x P/E.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $177.20 (20% Prob)
Flawless execution. IREN signs massive, high-margin, long-term HPC contracts with major hyperscalers, achieving a 90% revenue CAGR for 5 years. Net margins reach 25%. Dilution is kept to a more manageable 15% due to lucrative upfront tenant financing. The market awards a 35x P/E. The massive upside asymmetry in this scenario lifts the weighted fair value above the current share price.
