## Investment Thesis
PSIX is a compelling 'picks and shovels' play on the AI power bottleneck, offering deep value compared to highly speculative infrastructure peers.

1. **The Grid Bypass**: AI data centers require massive power that local grids simply cannot supply quickly enough. Hyperscalers are increasingly turning to on-site prime and standby power generation (natural gas, diesel, etc.) to get data centers online. PSIX manufactures these exact systems.
2. **Exceptional Fundamentals**: Unlike peers burning billions in cash, PSIX is already highly profitable. A Piotroski F-Score of 6 and a Rule of 40 score of 48.48 highlight excellent operational health. They grew revenue 32% while maintaining a ~16% net margin.
3. **Valuation Arbitrage**: The market is mispricing PSIX as a cyclical, low-margin industrial manufacturer (hence the 14x P/E). As the market realizes PSIX is critical infrastructure for the AI buildout, the multiple will expand significantly.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $112.80 (50% Prob)
Data center power generation demand drives consistent growth. PSIX achieves a 15% revenue CAGR over the next 5 years. Net margins stabilize at 16%.
