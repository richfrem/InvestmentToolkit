## Investment Thesis
BW is an explosive turnaround play transitioning from legacy industrial boilers to next-gen clean energy tech (BrightLoop hydrogen).

1. **The Turnaround Narrative**: The stock's 2952% surge indicates the market believes bankruptcy risk is completely off the table. BW has successfully restructured and is now positioned as a high-growth energy transition company.
2. **BrightLoop Potential**: The BrightLoop chemical looping technology is a potential game-changer for producing clean hydrogen and syngas. Analysts expect this to drive revenue from $587M today to $1.34B by 2027.
3. **Execution Risk**: The company is still burning cash (-$85M FCF). If the commercialization of BrightLoop stalls or encounters technical hurdles, the cash burn will quickly lead to dilution, destroying the turnaround narrative.
