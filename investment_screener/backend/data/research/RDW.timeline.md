## Investment Thesis

Redwire Corporation is a small-cap space infrastructure and defense technology company with genuinely compelling end-markets. Its sensors, star trackers, avionics, ISR payloads, and in-space manufacturing capabilities are precisely the technologies the Pentagon needs for missile defense, satellite constellation management, and Space Force expansion. The Golden Dome missile defense initiative — a $175B+ multi-year defense program — represents a real and large addressable market for Redwire's product portfolio.

The problem is price. At $24.57, the market is simultaneously pricing in Golden Dome contract wins, a dramatic turnaround from -67.6% net margins to profitability, sustained 24%+ revenue growth for 5 years, AND a premium P/E multiple on the terminal earnings. Each of these assumptions is aggressive individually; requiring all of them simultaneously makes the current price indefensible.

The fundamental trajectory is alarming. Gross margins have collapsed from a 2023 high of 23.8% to a 2025 low of 5.2% — nearly at the point where the company earns less per dollar of revenue than its direct material costs. FCF burn accelerated from -$28M in FY2024 to -$200M in FY2025, a 7x deterioration. The company was forced into a large mid-year equity offering in FY2025 (shares outstanding jumped from ~99M weighted avg to 198.9M current), yet even after raising significant capital, the burn rate remains unsustainable.

Analyst consensus confirms the fundamental picture: 9 analysts with a mean target of $14.33 and a high of $22 — both below today's price before this month's 167% rally. The forward P/E is -59.9x reflecting analyst consensus for continued losses through at least 2027. The stock is pricing in a transformation that has not yet begun to materialize.

## Key Risks

1. **Gross margin structural deterioration**: At 5.2% gross margin, the company is near the threshold where it loses money on every contract before even counting G&A or R&D. If this is structural (not just mix), profitability is years away even in an optimistic revenue environment.

2. **FCF cash burn exhausts capital**: -$200M FCF on $335M revenue = -60% FCF margin. At this rate, even a large equity raise provides only ~2-3 years of runway before the next dilutive offering. Each new offering resets the per-share value lower.

3. **Golden Dome is a narrative, not yet a contract**: The stock is pricing in contract awards that have not been announced. If program priorities shift, contracts are delayed, or RDW loses bids to BAE Systems, L3Harris, or Northrop Grumman, the narrative collapses.

4. **Momentum reversal risk**: With beta=2.42 and +167% in one month, a reversal to mean would be violent. The stock could give back most of this year's gains before fundamentals can provide a floor.

5. **Concentration and competition**: Small-cap defense contractors are vulnerable to losing a major contract that represents a disproportionate share of revenue. RDW competes against primes (Northrop, L3Harris) with far deeper resources on key DoD programs.
