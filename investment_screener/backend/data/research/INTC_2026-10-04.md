## Operating picture

Q2 2026 (reported 2026-07-23):

- Revenue $16.13B, up 25.4% year on year, about 12% above consensus.
- Non-GAAP gross margin 41.8%; non-GAAP EPS $0.42 against guidance of $0.20.
- GAAP loss of $2.16 per share, driven by a $12.53B non-cash CHIPS Act escrow charge. This is why the trailing net margin reads -19.8%.
- Data center and AI revenue $6.3B, up 59%. Client revenue $7.7B, with 27% higher average selling prices offsetting 8% lower volume.
- Foundry revenue $5.8B, operating loss $2.1B. External customers contributed $293M. No external 18A customer is named.
- Operating cash flow $7B; about $30B in cash and short-term investments.

Q3 2026 guidance: revenue $16.3B ($15.8B–$16.8B), non-GAAP gross margin 42.0%, non-GAAP EPS $0.38.

Consensus (43 analysts): FY26 revenue $63.1B, FY27 $72.0B; FY26 EPS $1.52, FY27 $2.06; mean target $116.37, median $112.00.

## Cross-checks

- **Discount-rate sensitivity**: $52.81 at 8.5%, $49.30 at 10%, $42.53 at the 13.3% computed WACC. The WACC uses a beta of 1.93 measured while the stock rose about 220%, so 10% was used as the primary rate.
- **Reverse DCF**: the current price implies about 40% annual revenue growth for five years at base-case margin and multiple — more than the bull case.
- **Monte Carlo** (5,000 runs): p10 $25.34, p50 $37.94, p90 $53.87.
- **Comps**: implied $26.72–$32.66, but this lens is unreliable. Only TSM and MU were usable, and the 3.1x EV/Sales median suggests a currency mismatch in TSM's revenue.

## What the price requires

To justify $119 today at a 10% return, year-5 earnings would need to be roughly $48B at a 22x multiple. Intel's record net income was about $21B. A more aggressive case than the bull scenario (25% growth, 25% margin, 30x, 1.2 quality) is worth about $180, so the price is not outside every plausible outcome — but it needs foundry success that is not yet contracted.

## What would change this view

- A named external 14A or 18A customer with committed volume.
- Foundry reaching operating break-even ahead of plan.
- Q3 2026 revenue and FY27 guidance well above the $72B consensus.

## Key risks to the valuation

- No named external foundry customer; foundry loses about $2.1B per quarter.
- Current pricing strength depends on supply constraints that management expects to ease in the second half of 2026.
- About 58x forward earnings.
- Share count rose from about 5.0B to 5.29B over the past year.
