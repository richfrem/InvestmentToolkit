## Investment Thesis
EQT stands out as a highly efficient operator in the cyclical oil & gas exploration sector. 

1. **Financial Fortress**: An exceptional Piotroski F-Score of 8 highlights comprehensive financial health across profitability, leverage, and operating efficiency. The company is fundamentally sound.
2. **Cash Flow Machine**: Producing $2.8B in Free Cash Flow gives management massive flexibility. They have the capital to execute meaningful share buybacks (modeling a 5% reduction in base case) and debt reduction, insulating the balance sheet from commodity price shocks.
3. **Structural Scale**: As the largest US natural gas producer, EQT benefits from massive economies of scale in the Appalachian Basin. This lowers their breakeven extraction costs, giving them a structural moat against smaller operators during cyclical downturns.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $71.00 (50% Prob)
Natural gas prices remain relatively stable. EQT achieves a modest 12% revenue growth rate as they optimize their asset base. Net margins hold strong at 25%. Management uses FCF to retire 5% of outstanding shares over 5 years. The market awards a reasonable 12x Exit P/E, standard for a mature, efficient energy producer.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $38.50 (30% Prob)

### 🚀 Bull Case: $107.00 (20% Prob)

Revenue compounds at 20% for 5 years, with net margins expanding to 35%. Aggressive buybacks reduce the share count by 10%. The market awards a 15x P/E.
