## Investment Thesis

Arista Networks has emerged as the premier provider of high-speed Ethernet networking solutions, directly challenging Cisco's legacy dominance in the data center. The explosion of Generative AI has created an insatiable demand for highly performant, low-latency networks to connect massive clusters of GPUs. Arista is a primary beneficiary of this trend, as evidenced by its 28.9% trailing revenue growth and exceptional profitability.

What separates Arista from commoditized hardware vendors is its Extensible Operating System (EOS) software. This software layer creates significant switching costs and allows Arista to command gross margins of ~64% and net margins of ~39%. The company generates immense free cash flow ($4.25B TTM) and has a flawless balance sheet, reflected in a high Piotroski F-Score of 6.

While the trailing P/E of 62.8x might initially screen as expensive, the forward P/E of ~40x provides a more accurate view of its earning power given the sheer velocity of AI infrastructure capex. Our DCF model conservatively assumes growth decelerates to a 22% CAGR (below the 24%+ blended analyst consensus) while expanding margins slightly to 40% as software scales. Even with a normalized 30x exit multiple, the intrinsic value of $198.76 offers a 15.1% margin of safety. Arista remains a core holding for AI infrastructure.
