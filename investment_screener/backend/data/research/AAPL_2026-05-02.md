## Investment Thesis

Apple's business model is a masterclass in ecosystem lock-in. With over 2 billion active devices globally, the company has successfully transitioned from a pure hardware vendor to a platform business where high-margin Services (App Store, iCloud, Apple Music, etc.) drive structural margin expansion. This is evident in their TTM net margin of 26.9% and massive $98.8B in trailing free cash flow.

While the Rule of 40 score of 51.38 is exceptional, it is heavily weighted by their immense profitability rather than high topline growth, as smartphone replacement cycles continue to elongate. The upcoming "Apple Intelligence" AI cycle provides a potential catalyst for a hardware upgrade supercycle, but this appears largely priced in at ~34x trailing earnings.

Our DCF model, which assumes a baseline 11% growth rate and consistent 3% annual share repurchases, yields a fair value of $269.82. This implies the stock is currently trading slightly above its intrinsic value (-3.7% upside). While Apple remains a core holding for most portfolios due to its extreme safety and unparalleled capital return program, it does not present an asymmetric buying opportunity at current levels.
