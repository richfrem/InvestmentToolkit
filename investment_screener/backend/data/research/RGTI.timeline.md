## Investment Thesis

Rigetti Computing is a legitimate quantum hardware company with real quantum processors (Ankaa-2, 79 qubits), a cloud QaaS platform, and 60+ patents in superconducting qubit fabrication. The technical work is genuine. The problem is the price.

$5.82B market cap on $7M *declining* revenue is not an investment thesis — it is a speculation thesis. The question is not "is quantum computing real?" (it is) but "will Rigetti specifically be a major commercial winner, and does $5.82B today represent fair compensation for that bet?"

The fundamental DCF answer is unambiguous: at $17.50, you are paying $16.45 above any reasonable present-value scenario for pure optionality. Even if Rigetti executes a spectacular commercial ramp (130% CAGR to $456M revenue, 15% GAAP margins by 2031), the present value is $4.63 — a 74% discount to current price.

The timing risk is severe: IBM, Google, and Microsoft all have quantum computing programs with dramatically more resources. AWS Braket aggregates multiple quantum hardware providers, reducing Rigetti's differentiation. If any one of these hyperscalers achieves quantum advantage first, Rigetti's commercial window narrows significantly.

## Key Risks

1. **Hyperscaler displacement**: IBM, Google, and Microsoft have billions in quantum R&D budgets. If any of them achieves quantum advantage with commercial applications, standalone quantum hardware vendors face existential competition. Rigetti cannot match their investment capacity.
2. **Funding cliff**: With no profitability and tiny revenue, Rigetti depends on capital markets to continue operations. A risk-off environment or quantum computing disappointment could trigger dilutive fundraising at much lower prices.
3. **Revenue declining in current quantum "hype" cycle**: The stock is near all-time highs while revenue is falling. This disconnect suggests market price is driven by quantum computing narrative momentum, not business performance.
4. **Technology execution risk**: Superconducting qubits face fundamental coherence time and error rate challenges. IonQ's trapped-ion approach may ultimately prove superior — Rigetti has bet on one technical path.
5. **Multiple compression**: The stock ran from ~$1.50 to $17+ in 6 months on quantum computing excitement (primarily Google Willow announcement). Momentum stocks that run on narrative are vulnerable to 80-90% corrections when narrative sentiment shifts.
