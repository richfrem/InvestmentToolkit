## Investment Thesis
CEG is the premier utility play on the AI data center boom, offering a unique combination of scale and clean energy.

1. **The Baseload Premium**: AI data centers require massive, uninterrupted, 24/7 power that intermittent renewables (solar/wind) cannot provide. As the largest operator of nuclear power plants in the US, CEG owns the exact asset class hyperscalers are desperate for.
2. **Lucrative PPAs**: Tech giants (Microsoft, Google, Meta) are aggressively signing long-term Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) at premium rates to secure carbon-free nuclear energy. This guarantees predictable, high-margin cash flows for CEG for decades.
3. **Financial Strength**: Unlike speculative energy startups, CEG is a mature utility generating $1.28B in free cash flow with a solid Piotroski F-Score of 5. While trading at a premium 22.5x forward P/E for a utility, the "AI power premium" is structurally justified by the supply-demand imbalance in the grid.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $357.00 (50% Prob)
CEG capitalizes on the data center boom by securing favorable long-term PPAs. Revenue grows at a steady 12% CAGR over 5 years. The premium pricing from tech clients pushes net margins up to 12%. Strong cash flow allows for a 2% share buyback. The market awards a 20x Exit P/E, a premium over legacy utilities but reasonable for an AI-adjacent infrastructure leader. 

### 🐻 Bear Case: $182.00 (30% Prob)
Grid constraints ease faster than expected or hyperscalers pivot to localized natural gas generation due to regulatory hurdles for nuclear. Revenue growth slows to 5% annually. Margins compress to 8% as wholesale power prices normalize. The market removes the "AI premium" and de-rates the stock to a traditional utility 15x P/E.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $542.00 (20% Prob)
The power bottleneck becomes severe. CEG commands astronomical premiums for its clean nuclear power. The company successfully brings offline reactors back onto the grid to meet demand. Revenue compounds at 18% for 5 years with net margins expanding to 15%. Aggressive buybacks reduce the share count by 5%. The market awards a 25x P/E.
