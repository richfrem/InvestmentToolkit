## Investment Thesis
SEI is executing a fascinating and high-growth pivot into the AI infrastructure space.

1. **The Power Solutions Pivot**: Formerly an oilfield logistics company, SEI is leveraging its equipment expertise to provide modular power generation and distribution solutions. The data center bottleneck is primarily power, and SEI's solutions offer a critical grid-bypass capability.
2. **The FCF Canyon**: The growth trajectory is undeniable, but the economics of this transition are extremely capital-heavy. The company burned $437M in free cash flow last year. 
3. **The Dilution Reality**: To fund this rapid expansion and support a $6.7B market cap without taking on toxic debt, SEI will have to issue equity. I model a 15% shareholder dilution in the base case. At 27x forward P/E, the market is pricing in a successful pivot and ignoring the dilutive impact.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $51.75 (45% Prob)
SEI successfully scales its Power Solutions division. Revenue compounds at a strong 35% CAGR over 5 years. Net margins reach a respectable 10%. However, the capital required to build out the equipment fleet forces a 15% dilution of the equity base. The market awards a 20x Exit P/E, treating it as a successful industrial transition story. The present value sits below the current trading price.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $6.68 (35% Prob)
Data center build-outs slow, or larger, better-capitalized incumbents push SEI out of the market. Growth drops to 10% annually. Margins stagnate at 5%. Heavy ongoing capex forces a painful 20% dilution at depressed stock prices. The market de-rates the stock to a 15x P/E, acting as a punishing multiple compression.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $211.00 (20% Prob)
Flawless execution. SEI becomes the standard for modular, rapid-deployment power solutions for hyperscalers. Revenue grows at an astonishing 55% CAGR for 5 years. Economies of scale drive net margins to 15%. Dilution is limited to 5% due to strong operating cash flows. The market awards a 25x P/E.
