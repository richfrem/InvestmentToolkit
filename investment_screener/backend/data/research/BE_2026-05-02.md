## Investment Thesis
Bloom Energy is arguably the purest "picks and shovels" play for the AI infrastructure energy bottleneck. As hyperscalers race to build gigawatt data centers, local utility grids cannot keep pace.

1. **The Energy Bottleneck**: AI data centers require massive, uninterrupted power. Bloom's fuel cells can be deployed on-site much faster than waiting for utility grid upgrades, making them a "must-have" for rapid deployment.
2. **Pricing Perfection**: A $82B market cap for a hardware manufacturer with $2B in revenue is unprecedented. The market assumes Bloom will scale linearly without supply chain hiccups or commodity inflation.
3. **Margin Inflection**: Bloom has historically burned cash, but recently crossed into positive margin territory (0.25%). To justify the valuation, they must reach and sustain mid-teen net margins, akin to premium industrial equipment monopolies.

## Scenario Analysis

### ⚖️ Base Case: $180.00 (45% Prob)
Bloom executes exceptionally well. Growth averages 60% annually over the next 5 years as hyperscalers standardize on their servers. Net margins expand to 15% as manufacturing scales. Exit P/E of 25 reflects a high-quality industrial compounder.

### 🐻 Bear Case: $8.00 (30% Prob)
Grid upgrades catch up or next-gen nuclear/SMR technologies become viable faster than expected. Growth slows to 30%, and pricing pressure limits net margins to 5%. Market realizes it's a cyclical hardware business, compressing the Exit P/E to 15.

### 🚀 Bull Case: $697.00 (25% Prob)
Bloom establishes a functional monopoly on off-grid AI power. Revenue compounds at an astonishing 80% for 5 years, reaching $38B. Operating leverage drives net margins to 20%. The market awards them a 35x P/E due to indispensable structural dominance.
