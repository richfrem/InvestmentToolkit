## Investment Thesis

Coinbase is the best-positioned regulated crypto exchange in the world. It survived the 2022 crash (when revenue fell 57% and net margins hit -82%), emerged leaner, and captured the 2024 bull market with $6.56B revenue and 39% net margins. The company has built something genuinely hard to replicate: a full regulatory compliance stack (SEC, CFTC, state money transmitter licenses, EU MiCA), institutional prime brokerage (Coinbase Prime), and a growing L2 blockchain (Base) that could become a durable platform revenue source.

The problem is timing and valuation. Revenue is already declining — down 22% YoY from the TTM peak. Crypto bull markets are typically followed by 18-36 month correction periods where trading volumes and fees compress dramatically. In 2022-2023, Coinbase revenue spent two full years at $3.1B — less than half of current TTM levels. At 38x forward P/E with falling revenue, the stock is pricing in conditions that ended before analysts revised their models.

The bear case (35% weight) is not Coinbase failing — it's Coinbase at crypto cycle trough, with 5% net margins and $5.6B revenue, at a 12x distressed-cyclical multiple. That produces a $7.57 present value. Combined with the base case ($98.16), the weighted result of $124 is 35% below current price.

Re-entry below $120 gives better risk/reward for the base case. Below $80, the bull case starts to provide meaningful margin of safety.

## Key Risks

1. **Crypto cycle timing**: The biggest risk to the SELL thesis is the crypto cycle turning faster than expected. If Bitcoin goes to $150K before year-end, COIN could re-rate to $300+ regardless of our DCF. Crypto cycles are driven by sentiment and ETF flows, not fundamentals.
2. **Revenue concentration**: ~75-80% of revenue is trading fees, which are directly correlated with crypto prices and volatility. A single volatile quarter can swing annual revenue by 40%+.
3. **Share count discrepancy**: API diluted shares (223M) vs NI/EPS implied (260M) = 16.6% gap. If actual diluted count is 260M, EPS is ~10% lower than modeled and all scenarios are more bearish.
4. **DEX competition**: Decentralized exchanges now handle $50B+ monthly volume. As gas costs fall with L2 scaling, centralized exchange advantages diminish for technically sophisticated users.
5. **Regulatory tail risk**: Despite Coinbase's compliance leadership, a coordinated international crypto regulatory action (e.g., staking banned as security) could eliminate an entire revenue stream.
