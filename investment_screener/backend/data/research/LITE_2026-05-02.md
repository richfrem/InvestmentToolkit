## Investment Thesis
Lumentum is executing a massive inflection point driven by the AI hardware supercycle.

1. **The Optical Bottleneck**: As GPU clusters scale to tens of thousands of units, the optical transceivers connecting them become the critical bottleneck. Lumentum's Cloud & Networking division is perfectly positioned to capture this massive capex wave from hyperscalers.
2. **Margin Inflection**: The company's recent results (Piotroski F-Score 7) show strong operational leverage. After significant net losses in previous years, they are transitioning to a positive margin trajectory. Analysts are modeling a tripling of revenue by 2027.
3. **Priced for Perfection**: At $67B, it trades at an aggressive ~41x trailing sales. This means the massive, multi-year growth curve is largely priced in today. Any execution misstep or slowdown in AI capex will result in severe multiple compression.
