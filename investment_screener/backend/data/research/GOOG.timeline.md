## Investment Thesis

### Core Thesis: AI Monetization Leader, Not Victim

Google's dominant narrative risk — that AI chatbots will destroy search — has overshadowed a more nuanced reality: Google is the primary beneficiary of the AI transition, not its casualty. AI Overviews now surface for over 50% of queries in the US and are generating **higher-CPC commercial interactions** as users shift from informational queries to transactional ones. The feared "zero-click" apocalypse has not materialized; instead, advertisers are paying premium CPCs for AI-assisted purchase-intent queries.

### Financial Quality: Top Decile

Alphabet's financial trajectory from 2021–2026 is exceptional by any measure:
- Net margins expanded from **21.2% → 37.9%** (TTM) — a 16.7pp improvement over 4 years driven entirely by operating leverage, not accounting changes
- Revenue 3-year CAGR of **12.5%** (growing *faster* recently: 21.8% YoY TTM)
- Free cash flow of **$72.8B** (2025 TTM), funding $70B+ in share repurchases
- Piotroski F-Score of **7/9** signals high financial quality: positive and improving ROA, strong CFO/NI ratio, declining leverage, expanding gross margins, improving asset turnover

### Google Cloud: The Underappreciated Growth Engine

Google Cloud grew ~28% YoY in 2025, reaching approximately $45B annualized revenue. While still trailing AWS (~$120B) and Azure (~$90B), Google Cloud has differentiated through:
1. **Vertex AI** — the only hyperscaler offering direct Gemini model access at inference scale
2. **BigQuery** — market-leading analytics with no true substitute in the enterprise segment
3. **Anthos/multi-cloud** — genuine differentiation vs AWS's walled garden

At current trajectories, Google Cloud reaches $80B+ in Year 3 of our model, becoming a material portion of total revenue (and at meaningfully improving margins).

### Waymo: Optional Value, Real Progress

Waymo is now operating commercially in San Francisco, Austin, Los Angeles, and Phoenix, with plans for 10+ additional cities. This remains an option value not captured in our base model, but a successful Waymo commercialization could represent $50–100B in standalone value by 2030.

## Key Risks

1. **AI Search Disruption** — If AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Apple AI) capture >20% of commercial queries within 3 years, Search CPCs collapse. This is the single most important risk and why the bear scenario receives 20% weight.

2. **DOJ Antitrust Remedies** — The DOJ won its antitrust case. Remedies under consideration include forced divestiture of Chrome, Android default search distribution, or the Play Store. Any forced divestiture would materially impair Google's distribution moat.

3. **AI Capex ROI** — Alphabet is spending $75B+ annually on AI/cloud infrastructure. If Gemini fails to capture enterprise AI market share at scale and Cloud growth decelerates below 20%, margin expansion stalls or reverses.

4. **YouTube Competitive Pressure** — TikTok (pending US resolution), Instagram Reels, and emerging short-form platforms compete aggressively for advertiser budgets. YouTube's market share of digital video advertising could compress from ~28% to ~22%.

5. **Macro/FX** — ~55% of revenue is international. USD strength reduces reported revenue. Economic downturns correlate with ad market contractions (Google's ad revenue dipped ~2% in Q2 2022).
