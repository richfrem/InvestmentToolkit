# Investment Thesis Proposal: Quality SaaS Resilience

**Date**: 2026-05-26
**Agent**: Portfolio Advisor
**Status**: APPROVED

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Established SaaS platforms with insurmountable distribution moats and deep enterprise integration are best positioned to monetize AI at the application layer through embedded agentic workflows.

**Key Metadata**:
- **Target Weight**: ~4.0%
- **Pillar**: Quality SaaS
- **Role**: core
- **Time Horizon**: 1-3 years

---

## 2. The Core Thesis (Why this? Why now?)

While the infrastructure layer captures the initial wave of AI spend, the application layer will ultimately capture the productivity value. Incumbents like Salesforce (Agentforce) and ServiceNow (AI workflow automation) possess the proprietary enterprise data required to ground AI agents. They don't need to invent the LLM; they just need to integrate it into workflows where switching costs are prohibitively high.

---

## 3. Adversarial Red-Team Review

- **Risk 1**: Disruptive AI-native startups build cheaper, faster workflows from scratch, bypassing legacy technical debt.
- **Risk 2**: AI coding agents allow internal enterprise IT teams to build custom workflow solutions, reducing reliance on expensive SaaS subscriptions.
- **Risk 3**: Margin compression as LLM API costs eat into software gross margins.

---

## 4. Valuation & Sizing Justification

- **Weighting Rationale**: Kept intentionally small (~4%) due to current valuation premiums on software and the prioritization of physical AI infrastructure.

---

## 5. Execution Plan

- **Entry Rules**: Accumulate selectively on earnings pullbacks.
- **Capital Source**: Dividend yields or trimming oversized infrastructure plays.
