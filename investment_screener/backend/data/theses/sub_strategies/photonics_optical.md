## 1. Executive Summary

The physical limits of copper are forcing a multi-decade infrastructure shift toward photonic (optical) interconnects inside data centers. AI training clusters — which require 400G+ bandwidth between GPUs over distances of meters to kilometers — cannot be economically served by copper beyond ~5 meters at those speeds. Silicon photonics enables optical components at semiconductor-scale volumes and costs, collapsing what was once a telecom-only technology into commodity data center hardware.

## 2. The Core Thesis (Why this? Why now?)

**The copper wall**: At 400G and above, copper interconnects (NRZ/PAM4) reach fundamental physical limits. Signal integrity degrades, power consumption explodes, and reach drops below 3-5 meters. Every hyperscaler building AI clusters (NVIDIA GB200 NVL racks, Google TPU pods, Microsoft Maia) is hitting this wall simultaneously.

**Co-packaged optics (CPO)**: NVIDIA, Broadcom, and Intel are integrating optical transceivers directly into switch ASICs — eliminating the pluggable transceiver market over time but dramatically expanding total optical silicon volume. COHR, LITE, and POET all supply components into this ecosystem.

**Supply chain leverage**: Unlike consumer electronics, optical components require specialty substrates (InP, GaAs — AXTI), precision glass fiber (GLW), and high-speed connectors (APH). These supply-chain moats are durable, low-profile, and often overlooked.

**Margin expansion story**: Optical component companies have historically operated at low margins due to telecom overcapacity. AI data center demand is a new, high-urgency customer segment paying premium ASPs. Margin expansion from 2-5% to 12-18% is the multi-year story as product mix shifts from legacy telecom to hyperscaler AI.

- **Key Pure-Play Players**: COHR (800G/1.6T transceivers), LITE (laser components, 3D sensing), AAOI (DC transceivers), CRDO (silicon photonics PHY, AEC), CIEN (optical networking), POET (photonic integrated circuits)
- **Supply Chain**: GLW (glass fiber), APH (fiber connectors), AXTI (InP/GaAs substrates), VIAV (optical components + test)
- **ETF Access**: FOTO (photonics sector basket)
